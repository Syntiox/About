Milestones

Our journey so far.

Key moments that shaped Syntiox into what it is today.

2024 — Foundation

Syntiox Founded

Started as a vision to build enterprise-grade technology from Sri Lanka. The initial focus was on AI and security research.

2024 — First Products

Syntiox AI & Atom Launched

Launched our flagship AI platform and Atom — a markdown editor built for developers and researchers.

2024 — Expansion

API Console & Game Store

Expanded the ecosystem with a developer-focused API console and an innovative game store platform.

2025 — Infrastructure

Syntiox Lab & Pay

Launched Syntiox Lab for experimental research and Syntiox Pay as a secure payment gateway — solidifying our infrastructure.

2025+ — The Future

Scaling Globally

Continuing to expand our product ecosystem, grow the team, and prove that world-class innovation can come from anywhere.
